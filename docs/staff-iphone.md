# Tekmadev Admin on iPhone

Send the text below to staff as it is.

---

Until the iPhone app is ready, use Tekmadev Admin from your home screen:

1. Open Safari and go to tekmadev.com/admin
2. Tap Share (the square with the arrow). If you don't see it, tap the ... button first.
3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, keep "Open as Web App" on, and tap Add.
4. Open Tekmadev from your home screen and sign in with your work email and password. Sign in inside the app even if you already did in Safari. You only do this once.

It opens on Leads. Add lead is the button in the middle at the bottom. Follow-ups shows what is due today and earlier.

Calls, texts and emails open your phone's own apps. When you come back, tap "Log this call" (it waits on the lead, or at the top of Leads, for 2 hours).

To see the newest data, tap Refresh on the list or on a lead, or leave the app and come back.

Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password?", open the email link, set a new password, then go back to the Tekmadev icon and sign in.

---

## For the owner

- Add it from `/admin`, not the public home page, or the icon opens the public site.
- The name and icon are fixed when added: after changing them, remove and re-add the icon.
- The app keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.
- There are no push alerts on iPhone yet: the bell shows new items while the app is open.
